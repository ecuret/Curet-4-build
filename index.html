Site Purpose

Welcome to your go-to guide for unlocking the full nutritional benefits in seeds, grains, nuts, and legumes through a process known as sprouting. Many raw plant foods contain a compound known as phytic acid, which is a natural compound that blocks your body from absorbing key minerals like iron, zinc, and calcium. This site was created to break down the simple science of how soaking and sprouting the aforementioned items breaks down their phytic acid content, which makes these foods much easier to digest. Whether you are a beginner or looking to improve your kitchen routine, you will find clear step-by-step sprouting guides, and basic tips to help you get the most nutrition out of every bite.

Sprouting Guide

As stated above, soaking and sprouting nuts breaks down phytic acid and enzyme inhibitors, which improves digestibility and increases nutrient absorption. I'm pretty sure you don't know where to begin in order to begin your sprouted food journey; however, allow me to be your trusty guide. I've summarized some steps below to help you understand exactly how to go about doing this, if you ever wanted to attempt this on your own.

🌱Sprouting Steps:🌱

  1. When beginning, always choose raw nuts. This is a default choice because only unroasted, raw nuts can be properly soaked and sprouted. Note: that most true tree nuts (like walnuts, pecans, cashews, and hazelnuts) only require soaking, whereas almonds—which are technically seeds—will grow a visible sprout. (If sprouting almonds, look for unpasteurized or non-irradiated varieties so they can germinate.)
  2. Rinse thoroughly. Place your raw nuts in a fine-mesh strainer and rinse them under cool running water to remove surface dust and debris.
  3. Submerge in salted water. Transfer the nuts to a glass bowl or jar and cover them completely with warm filtered water (about a 2:1 water-to-nut ratio, as nuts will expand). Stir in roughly 1 tablespoon of sea salt per cup of nuts to help neutralize enzyme inhibitors.
  4. Soak for the right duration. Cover the container loosely with a clean towel or mesh lid and leave it at room temperature. Soaking times vary by nut density:
    5. • Cashews and macadamias: 2 to 4 hours
    • Walnuts and pecans: 4 to 6 hours
    • Almonds and hazelnuts: 8 to 12 hours
  5. Drain and rinse again. Pour the nuts into a colander, discard the murky soaking water, and rinse the nuts well with fresh water. At this point, soak-only nuts are ready to eat immediately or blend into nut milks and sauces.
  6. Sprout (if applicable). To sprout almonds, place the drained nuts in a jar topped with a mesh sprouting lid or cheesecloth. Prop the jar at an angle in a dark, well-ventilated spot so excess water drains out. Rinse and drain the nuts 2 to 3 times daily for 1 to 3 days until tiny white sprout tails appear.
  7. Dry or store properly. Because moist nuts spoil quickly, store freshly soaked or sprouted nuts in an airtight container in the refrigerator and eat them within 3 to 4 days. For a crunchy snack and longer pantry shelf life, spread the nuts on a baking sheet or dehydrator tray and dry them at a low temperature (115°F to 150°F) for 12 to 24 hours until completely crisp.

Living Nutz

Livingnutz is an independent, Midcoast Maine-based food company founded in 2002 that specializes in raw, certified organic, sprouted nuts, seeds, and stone-ground nut butters. The company focuses on a lot of methods regarding the consumption of nuts and seeds, but my favorite aspect of them are their sprouted products. The company achieves their sprouted products by doing the following:

Sprouting and Dehydration. Instead of roasting, Living Nutz soaks (germinates) its nuts and seeds in alkaline deep-well water to reduce enzyme inhibitors, marinates them in sweet or savory seasonings, and slowly dehydrates them at low temperatures to preserve nutrients.

Lark Ellen Farm

Lark Ellen Farm is an organic, grain-free food company founded in 2015 by Kelley Scanlin in Ojai, California. It started out as a small food-stand called Ojai Granola Girl at the Ojai Farmers Market and subsequently grew out of a personal quest to heal chronic inflammation through diet. Today, the brand produces organic sprouted granolas, trail mixes, and sprouted nuts and seeds, and became the first sprouted nut and seed company to earn the Clean Label Project Purity Award. Visit the site link above to check out some of their products.

GoRaw

GoRaw is an organic, plant-based snack brand founded in 2002 that specializes in sprouted seeds, granola, and crisps. Rather than baking or frying, the company mimics natural germination by soaking seeds in water for 12 to 16 hours and gently dehydrating them at low temperatures to preserve nutrients and create a light crunch. They're probably the easiest to find in nutritional stores like Whole Foods, Fresh Market, Sprouts, and other similar health-conscious food markets.
